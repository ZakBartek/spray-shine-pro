# Mr-Spray Renewal Project

Create a website for: Mr-Spray – Professional Roof Painting & Maintenance

https://www.dachy-malowanie.pl/

The main services are professional roof painting, roof cleaning and maintenance, anti-corrosion protection, industrial painting, high-altitude work, cleaning of facades and paving, and photovoltaic installation.

Need an animated, modern 3D website and create separate pages like home page, about us, services, projects/realizations and contact us + create the contact form.

This should be a high converting website designed to convert the maximum amount of website visitors into leads plus it should be SEO friendly.

The homepage should contain:

an image gallery of past projects / before and after transformations – put in placeholders for now and I'll provide more photos later

a testimonials section – use real information from Google reviews if available

minimum 1,000 words of high-quality SEO-friendly content across the homepage

a clear explanation of the main services and benefits

strong call-to-action sections encouraging visitors to request a free quote or call the company

a section explaining why professional roof painting is a more affordable alternative to replacing a roof

a simple explanation of the process: inspection → cleaning and preparation → protection/anti-corrosion treatment → professional spray painting → finished result

service area information, focusing on Podkarpackie, Małopolskie and Śląskie

Design the entire site to look premium, modern and extremely professional. It should feel trustworthy and visually impressive while still being easy to navigate and focused on generating leads.

Get inspiration from high-end American contractor and home service websites. Use large cinematic visuals, subtle animations, modern typography, strong contrast and a premium color palette inspired by roofing and construction: deep charcoal/black, white and a strong accent color such as red or orange.

For the hero section, use a powerful background image or video of a professional working on a roof. The headline should immediately communicate the transformation and value of the service, for example:

"ODMIENIMY WYGLĄD TWOJEGO DACHU"

or another stronger Polish headline focused on roof transformation, protection and durability.

Include a prominent CTA button: "DARMOWA WYCENA" and a secondary CTA such as "ZOBACZ REALIZACJE".

The navigation should include:
Home | O firmie | Usługi | Realizacje | Kontakt

Create individual pages for the most important services, including:

Malowanie dachów

Malowanie blachodachówki

Antykorozja

Prace wysokościowe

Fotowoltaika

Malowanie przemysłowe

Pozostałe usługi

Make the website fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. Use smooth scroll animations and subtle interactive elements, but don't sacrifice loading speed or usability.

The business details are:

Company name: Mr-Spray
Specialization: Professional roof painting, cleaning and maintenance
Address: Groble 115D, 37-430 Jeżowe, Poland
Phone: +48 787 931 779
Website: https://www.dachy-malowanie.pl/

The company should be positioned as experienced, reliable and professional, using specialist equipment and modern spray-painting techniques. Emphasize quality, durability, attention to detail and professional customer service.

The primary goal of the website is to generate phone calls and quote requests.

Make the final result look significantly more modern and premium than a typical Polish construction company website. The website should build trust immediately and make the visitor want to contact the company.
